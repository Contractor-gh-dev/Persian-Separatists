Location: In orbit above New Umbra
Date: 193.12.26

The Persean Separatists have long evaded their time of reckoning, but they could not avoid the wrath of the Hegemony forever. Now that day has come.

In need of a more distrubuted industrial base and additional ships for the Second AI War, the Hegemony have moved fleet into position to take New Umbra, a planet housing the Separatists main manufacturing facilities. Should the planet fall, the rest of Separatist space would follow, bolstering Hegemony power and finally putting an end to the 'upstart rebels' once and for all.

To the PSM however, this is do or die for the entire polity. Every ship available has been called into service, from humble combat freighters, to the best fighting cruisers, and even a newly minted Independence class battleship.

Much in the way of supplies, ships, and lives will be lost, but the hammer is already falling. The battle over New Umbra has begun.
